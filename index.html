How to play

Find

Tap SCOPE, then drag to look around. Slide the zoom bar for more magnification. Hostiles wear black with a red armband and carry rifles.

Steady

Your breathing moves the crosshair. Hold BREATH to lock it still for a few seconds. Run out and the sway gets worse for a moment.

Drop and wind

Real gravity and air drag: rounds slow down and drop faster the farther they go, and wind pushes them more the longer they fly. The rifle is zeroed at 300 m. HOLD 1.5 UP means put the crosshair 1.5 dots above the target. Past about 600 m, range the target and tap DIAL to set the scope for that distance, like a real sniper. Flags show the wind.

Spot and distract

Hold the crosshair on anyone for a second to spot them: hostiles get a marker, civilians get labeled. With markers off (or in Veteran) that's the only way to tag targets. White ! markers are distractions: shoot a car alarm or a bottle and nearby guards walk over to look, without raising the alarm.

Stay quiet

Kills and near misses spook nearby hostiles. A spooked hostile runs for cover, and if one gets away the contract fails.
